Am I Compromised? Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

The short version: Everything is scanned on your own device. Am I Compromised? collects no personal information, has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and never uploads your logs, backups or results.

1. Information we collect

None. There is no sign-in, and the app never asks for your name, email address, phone number or location. We do not operate a server for the app.

2. What the app downloads

To stay current, the app downloads two public lists: spyware indicators from the mvt-indicators project and the repositories it lists, and a list of exploited Apple software bugs from AmICompromised-data. Both are hosted on GitHub. Like any website, GitHub receives your IP address when the files are downloaded; nothing about you or your device is sent with the request.

3. Logs and diagnostic files

When you share a sysdiagnose, or choose a log file or folder, the app reads it on your device to look for known spyware traces. The file is not uploaded, copied anywhere else or kept after the scan. If you use the Share sheet's "Scan for Spyware" action, the file is copied into the app's private storage until the scan finishes, then deleted.

4. iPhone backups (Mac)

The Mac app can read an iPhone backup made by Finder, or make one using Apple's own backup tool. Backups stay on your Mac. If a backup is encrypted, you type its password into the app; the password is used only in memory to unlock the backup for that scan and is never stored or sent anywhere.

5. Scanning your Mac (full version)

The full Mac version reads system settings, the list of running programs, startup items and logs, using Apple's built-in tools. This happens entirely on your Mac and nothing is sent anywhere.

6. Results stored on your device

Your last scan results, your theme choice and checklist ticks are saved on your device so you can see them later. They never leave the device and are deleted when you delete the app.

7. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app uses local notifications only (for example, "your sysdiagnose should be ready"). No push notification service is used.

8. Tips

The app is free. Optional tips are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details or your Apple Account.

9. Third parties

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting or social-media code. We do not sell or share any data, because we do not collect any.

10. Children's privacy

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

11. Deleting your data

There is no account to delete. Deleting the app removes everything it saved.

12. Changes to this policy

If the way the app handles data ever changes, this page will be updated and the date above revised. The app is open source, so you can also check the code yourself.

13. Contact
