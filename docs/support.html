Think you're being targeted right now? Contact the free, 24/7 Access Now Digital Security Helpline or Amnesty International's Security Lab. Don't erase your phone first: experts may need it as it is.

How do I scan my iPhone?

  1. Open the app, tap Spyware Log Scan and stay on that screen.
  2. Press both volume buttons and the side button together for about one second. The screen flashes.
  3. Wait up to 10 minutes. The app sends a notification when the file should be ready.
  4. Open Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements › Analytics Data, search for "sysdiagnose", tap the newest file, tap Share, then tap Scan for Spyware.

How do I scan my iPhone from my Mac?

Full Mac version: connect the iPhone with a cable, open the iPhone section and click Back Up and Scan. Unlock the iPhone and tap Trust if asked. The first time, allow Full Disk Access when the app asks (System Settings › Privacy & Security › Full Disk Access).

Mac App Store version: back up the iPhone in Finder (turn on Encrypt local backup for a deeper scan), then in the iPhone section click Choose Backup Folder.

The app found something. What now?

A match means something on your device appears on a published list of spyware traces. It's a strong lead, not proof. Keep the evidence (don't delete the sysdiagnose or backup), update your software, turn on Lockdown Mode, and contact one of the free helplines above for an expert check.

My result is clean. Am I safe?

It means no known traces were found today. New spyware has no public traces until researchers discover it, so no app can prove a device is clean. Keep your software updated, restart your phone regularly, and use Lockdown Mode if you might be targeted.

Why does the app need a sysdiagnose?

iOS doesn't let any app look inside other apps or the system. A sysdiagnose is a bundle of system logs that you create and choose to share, which is where traces of past spyware have been found.
